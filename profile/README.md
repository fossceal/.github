## 🚀 Vision

The vision of **FOSS CEAL** is to encourage and promote the use of **Free and Open Source Software (FOSS)** and related areas such as **Open Hardware**, among students and teachers of the institution, for every possible kind of use.

---

## 🏛 Chambers of FOSS CEAL

> [!NOTE]
> An initiative under **FOSS CEAL**, established in **2024**

FOSS CEAL is structured into **five specialized chambers**, each focusing on a core domain:

- 🎨 **CREATE101** — Manages Design, UI/UX, and creative technologies  
- 🔌 **EMBED202** — Manages Embedded systems, IoT, and hardware hacking  
- 🎓 **TRAIN303** — Manages Websites and AI related things
- 💻 **HACK404** — Manages Gaming, Ideathons, and hackathons  
- 🚀 **DEPLOY505** — Manges DevOps, cloud, and self-Hosting
